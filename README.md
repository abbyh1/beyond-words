# Beyond Words

**A self-guided chronic pain journaling toolkit built with React.**

Beyond Words explores how visual mapping, reflection, and storytelling can help people express the lived experience of chronic pain. Developed through NYU's Undergraduate Summer Research Program, the project brings human-computer interaction research into an interactive web application.

## Why Beyond Words?

Pain is personal and can be difficult to put into words. A numerical pain rating captures only part of an experience: it may leave out the circumstances, emotions, care strategies, and meaning surrounding it.

Beyond Words explores a more expressive approach. Its toolkit invites people to describe pain experiences in their own terms and reflect at their own pace, with an emphasis on gentle, nonjudgmental interaction.

## The Toolkit

The project centers on three complementary activities:

- **Visual Pain Episode Map:** Map an experience through a sequence of prompts covering triggers, feelings, the body, care, reflection, and insight.
- **Reflection Card:** Reflect on an episode through guided prompts.
- **Personal Narrative:** Use storytelling to give context and meaning to a pain experience.

These activities are intended to support personal expression rather than require a single, standardized way of describing pain.

## Design Approach

- **Expression over precision:** Make room for subjective experiences, metaphor, and storytelling.
- **Gentle language:** Use prompts that invite reflection without judgment.
- **User choice:** Let people engage with what feels relevant and comfortable.
- **Research-informed interaction:** Translate ideas from HCI and self-tracking research into concrete interface decisions.
